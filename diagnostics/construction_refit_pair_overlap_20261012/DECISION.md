# Decision and negative-evidence boundary

**`NO_GO_REUSE` for direct reuse of existing construction nominal outputs as the frozen bit-exact numeric-refit interval result.** Confidence is high for source-contract mismatch and current lifetime, and exact for captured pair identity/counts. This is not a rejection of every possible cross-stage co-design mechanism.

The frozen thresholds require BOTH identity and numeric-compatible fractions >=80% for STRONG_OVERLAP, and <50% on either denies current-output reuse. Identity passes. Source-contract admission including the optimistic self-zero exception is only 0.22218889% of unique refit pairs (0.22222% of tasks), so the declared direct-reuse gate fails. This classification must not be reported as measured zero nonself numeric agreement or an impossibility theorem for wider enclosures.

## Retained negative evidence

- Target/keeper: current P2, GIST N1M/D960/FP32, fixed 336-event trace, both actual rebuilds.
- Rejected mechanism: preserve/undo encoded legacy nominal distances and directly feed the unchanged directed-rounding interval refit result.
- Counterevidence: FP32-vs-FP64 subtraction; ordinary/FP32-vs-directed accumulation and sqrt; nominal scalar/encoded key vs two-endpoint safety envelope; all scoring output freed before consumer; different owner destinations for every scoring match.
- Added costs a future mechanism must charge: new sidecar writes/storage, pair/owner mapping, reductions, synchronization, numeric-path/order effects and remaining refit work. No such costs were measured here.
- Measured effect: no performance experiment and no observed latency benefit; identity counts only.
- Do-not-revive boundary: pair overlap alone, approximate nominal agreement, or scaling inversion cannot promote direct reuse under the same interval/array contract.

## Next action / reopen condition

Do not write a cache/fusion kernel from this result. Under the requested plan, return to range traversal/verification or another measured physical maintenance dependency. A separately proposed build-produced certificate could reopen this boundary only after a **narrow prior-art Gate 0**, a frozen arithmetic/owner/lifetime contract, and a cheap correctness test preserving the required query outputs and interval safety (bit-exact arrays if that remains required). Engineering packaging or small gains cannot rescue a subsumed thesis. This task neither authorizes nor runs that new direction.
