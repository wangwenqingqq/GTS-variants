# Decision

**NO_GO_LOCAL_MAINTENANCE**, exact frozen trace and P2 only.

- Implementation: observation-only code; no production path or keeper change.
- Local selective/incremental hypothesis: rejected by 99.982% / 99.985% changed
  canonical leaf memberships and 100% changed node topology in both rebuilds.
- Thesis impact: sparse logical mutation does cause dense physical maintenance,
  but that alone is not a redundancy claim. Most tree state actually changes.
- Separate opportunity: 99.999% of POST packed coordinate content is identical
  under occurrence alignment, despite physical row churn. No proven speedup or
  correct delta representation exists in this diagnosis.

Do not revive ordinary selective/local execution merely from the large
logical-denominator amplification. Reopen only with evidence of update-stable
organization or a clearly defined independently redundant representation whose
reuse preserves semantics. Before an expensive mechanism experiment, identify
nearest prior art and run its novelty kill test. No legacy incremental/Safe-C1
port, threshold change, external rerun or new workload was started.

Confidence is exact saved-state counting for two actual rebuilds after full
behavior parity; generalization is limited to one N1M D960 trace, one keeper,
one GPU. Deterministic CPU replay is not repeated GPU construction, a sanitizer
result, causality proof or performance evidence.
