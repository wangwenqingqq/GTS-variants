# Gate0: diagnostic, not a novel indexing contribution

Checked primary sources on2026-10-12. PCA, dimensionality sweeps, projected
lower bounds and exact filter/refine are established. This experiment is a cheap
finite-workload diagnosis, not an attempt to rescue a subsumed new-algorithm thesis.
No paper prose or expensive GPU campaign is authorized by a positive outcome.

Inference/decision: proceed only to the declared CPU information-capacity test.
Any follow-on information compressor or search-aware representation must face a
fresh nearest-prior-art kill test and a reproducible same-contract advantage.
A CAPACITY_LIMITED label cannot establish cheap compression; a PCA_DIRECTION_LIMITED
label cannot prove an unmeasured objective superior. HIGH_DIMENSION_INTRINSIC is
only the protocol bucket name, not an intrinsic-dimension estimate or impossibility
of nonlinear representations.
