Cheap novelty kill test: remove the certificate terminology. The proposed
max-spread, sorted equal-count recursive construction reduces to established
spatial partitioning. **The generic PCA/balanced-partition novelty claim is
rejected before experiments.** The authorized experiment remains useful as an
offline attribution test with fixed metadata, basis, scores and region sizes.
A positive result would not be the first proof that partition and representation
interact. A negative result would reject this split under this contract, not all
linear partitions or establish the necessity of nonlinear representations.
No new paper thesis is promoted here. A future certificate-effectiveness
construction objective requires its own prior-art and same-contract evaluation.
