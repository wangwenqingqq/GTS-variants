# Novelty decision

## CONDITIONAL

**The generic thesis is KILL; the narrowly frozen joint contract is only conditionally eligible for differentiation design. It is not a novelty PASS.**

### Established facts

1. Metric construction distances already form pruning metadata in VP/MVP, GNAT, and PM-tree. Pinned OMPL source demonstrates same-matrix assignment and bound updates. KILL-1 and KILL-2 apply to the generic recipe.
2. GPU spatial construction integrates topology and bounds. Karras still uses a post-topology bounding stage; GPU kd-tree has several bound-related passes. Therefore KILL-3 means HIGH system-novelty risk, not that every prior builder literally removes all refit kernels.
3. Directed arithmetic and separating nominal construction from certified numerical decisions are known numerical capabilities/designs.
4. The parent GTSPP diagnosis establishes exact pair overlap for two captured rebuilds, but current nominal values cannot be directly admitted to the frozen interval contract. No new arithmetic or timing was evaluated here.

### Candidate differences: established requirements, not established contributions

| Required difference | Current evidence | Judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Same build/refit pair identity | 100% on the captured GTSPP rebuilds; classical builders also reuse computed pairs for bounds | Useful attribution, insufficient novelty |
| Preserve native FP32 topology and produce directed FP64 certificates | Required by the baseline; not shown jointly in the checked sources | Unknown joint prior coverage; no PASS-B proof |
| One-level ephemeral certificate ownership, post-split child reduction | Proposed; local matrices and bound propagation already known | Must differentiate from ordinary sidecars/reduction; no PASS-C proof |

### Why neither PASS nor a full joint-contract KILL?

PASS would mistake failure to find an exact named combination for independent novelty. The strongest explanation is still **classical construction-bound reuse composed with standard directed arithmetic**. Exact topology/endpoint compatibility is a correctness constraint, not automatically a research contribution.

A full KILL of every safe producer would overstate the checked evidence: no nearest source here demonstrably supplies all the frozen numerical and ownership requirements together. Therefore the honest result is CONDITIONAL with HIGH risk, permitting only a cheap differentiation design. If that design reduces to ordinary per-level sidecars plus reduction, KILL the research direction **before GPU work**. Small gains or extra implementation cannot rescue a subsumed thesis.
