# Pre-experiment novelty boundary

**Admission: DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY_NOT_A_NEW_ROUTING_MECHANISM.**
The requested headroom test measures one fixed pool; it does not propose a new
dimension-ordering algorithm. Prior work already covers the central primitives.
A favorable result cannot admit CUDA or a manuscript claim in this task.

| Primary source | Established overlap | Boundary for this diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| [BOND, 2002](https://www.cs.uoi.gr/~nikos/sigmod02.pdf), Sections 5, 7.2 | Query-dependent dimension order, exact partial-distance pruning and independent column access. | A fixed 128-coordinate survivor comparison is a workload diagnostic, not a new partial-distance idea. |
| [PDX / PDX-BOND, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.04422v1), Section 5 | Exact pruning with query-aware dimension priority using dimension means; block-vertical storage addresses otherwise-unused coordinates fetched by horizontal storage. | Adding population variance to a mean-based score is not by itself a defensible non-incremental mechanism. Here the layout remains unchanged and physical traffic is unmeasured. |
| [DCO survey and experimental evaluation, EDBT 2026](https://openproceedings.org/2026/conf/edbt/paper-270.pdf), Sections 3, 5 | Reviews partial-dimension optimizers and evaluates their effectiveness and overhead across vector-search workloads. | Its external results are not adopted as our fixed-query measurements. |

The oracle reads all dimensions and selects separately for every pair. It is an
unpriced information diagnostic, not a deployable 512-byte search method. C1 is
the one specified moment-score probe; no alternative heuristic, subset-budget
sweep or new layout will be added. We test positive and negative feasibility
hypotheses without treating missing evidence as a negative result.

Only a numerical C1 GO would trigger the requested dedicated
`PRIOR_ART_ROUTING.md`. That audit must reject a subsumed thesis even if the cheap
diagnostic looks favorable. No GPU implementation is authorized by this plan.
Primary snapshots and download identities remain private; source PDFs are not
redistributed. This is not an exhaustive absence-of-prior-art claim.
