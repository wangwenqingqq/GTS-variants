# Validation scope

Executed work is primary-source review and an exact-arithmetic CPU fixture.
The registered 1,600 descriptor pairs and 6,400 cutoff checks were reproduced
with identical receipts. The zero false-rejection count applies to that synthetic
fixture only; no CIFAR result, training seed or floating-point kernel is qualified.

The standalone check verifies the contract/fixture/source hashes, unchanged parent
references, null measurement receipts and separation of admission from empirical
performance. Both entry points reject optimized Python before assertions can be
removed. A fresh archive is used to verify the exact published source set; no
third-party package, dataset, GPU initialization or upstream setup is required.

An independent read-only review challenged the novelty stop, checked its limited
HN norm assumptions, source evidence, arithmetic and null outputs. It did not
train models or replicate the author's performance. A source-review pass is not
an empirical admission of a new representation-learning method.

Publication adds only this task-owned directory. Credentials, raw conversations,
private operational placement/configuration, full third-party source/PDFs, images,
checkpoints and the confidential manuscript are excluded. Existing public history
is preserved; its old operational-path/contact findings are not relabelled as a
fully anonymous history. No history rewrite, unrelated cleanup or merge is made.

The three-way training-quality/cost gate remains NOT_EVALUATED_NOVELTY_STOP.
The parent Gate3/Gate4 decisions are unchanged and supply no new training numbers.
