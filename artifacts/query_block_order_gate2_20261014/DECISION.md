## Reopen condition

A future research admission needs an actual non-incremental mechanism, a
prospectively frozen complete numerical/cost contract, and decisive same-contract
evidence. Do not revive the thesis by changing only block size, predictor,
platform, estimator or dataset. If separately requested, a baseline/engineering
study may test useful acceleration without labeling it a new contribution; that
is not authorized by this closed research gate.

If a later phase is proposed, first resolve the denominator and gray/overlapping
gate intervals listed in CONTRACT.json. Cross-order FP64 and full exact-score
replay/retention costs must be frozen before bytes are compared; GPU numbers
require a separate resource, quality and same-campaign measurement registration.
