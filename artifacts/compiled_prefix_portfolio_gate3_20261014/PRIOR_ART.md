## Admission matrix and retained differences

The target flow is a small set of precomputed coordinate-subspace access paths,
query-dependent selection, a conservative distance filter, and original-object
refinement. The Euclidean index-striping evidence directly establishes that
core chain with scheduling rather than CPV4's one-time routing. Tree Striping
also explicitly allows first-index filtering followed by raw verification.
Workload-aware physical design supplies the portfolio-selection connection,
but is not falsely presented as a complete vector-search implementation.

CPV4 retains real differences: possibly overlapping128-D subsets instead of a
disjoint full-space decomposition; K=4; training-only greedy survivor coverage;
one moment-based route; dense contiguous FP32 buffers instead of trees; fixed
range rather than incremental kNN; and a potential GPU memory-traffic benefit.
None of their literal implementation identities is established in these sources.
One-time routing is a restricted access-path policy, and materializing the
chosen coordinates changes storage/locality, not the rejection information or
original-object refinement principle. The compiler's objective is a standard
best-access-path portfolio objective with a search-specific cost. This is an
analysis of the proposed mechanism, not a measured implementation comparison.

Accordingly, simply replacing the online traversal with a view ID does not
establish a non-incremental research mechanism under this plan. The family-level
stop is an inference from the direct full-space similarity architecture and the
physical-design comparison, **not** an assertion that one paper publishes the
entire K=4/compiler/router/GPU tuple. No storage-efficient realization or useful
acceleration is disproved. A capacity win alone would not establish novelty.

## Search boundary and stop

The bounded search covered multi-layout/replica physical design, materialized
projections, index portfolios/routing, partial VA-files, subspace multi-index
search, query-specific layouts and BOND/PDX physical orders. Approximate hash
search, metadata routing, distributed peer routing, and secondary descriptions
were not used as direct subsumption evidence. A partial VA-file is not silently
equated with four full-precision sidecars. No exhaustive absence claim is made.

Stop before building the training pool or running32M pairs. Portfolio entropy,
held-out survivors, oracle-route capacity, moment-route regret, actual sidecar
traffic, update latency and speedup remain UNKNOWN/NOT_RUN. Only exact-rational
payload/source algebra is checked; it is neither candidate qualification nor
the automatic verifier of this literature judgment.
