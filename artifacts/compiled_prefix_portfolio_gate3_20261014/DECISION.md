## Separate dispositions

- Mechanism admission: SUBSUMED_PORTFOLIO, inferred core-family scope.
- Implementation: no real-data pool, greedy compile, router, sidecar or GPU.
- Capacity/quality/traffic/latency: NOT_RUN, not zero errors or a measured fail.
- Cost algebra: exact source-model thresholds and payload arithmetic only.
- Thesis: no new novelty/performance claim; prior keeper evidence is unchanged.

The model reads512MB of prefix coordinates and3840B for each surviving raw row.
Full raw fallback deliberately rereads prefix coordinates; no free retention or
reconstruction is assumed. This is not a DRAM upper bound or end-to-end result.
Router, compaction, fields, metadata, residency and update coordination remain
unmeasured. The design storage gate applies to vector payload, not total memory.

## Reopen boundary

Do not rescue the thesis by changing only K, block size, router, device or cost
estimator. A genuinely different representation/certificate/ownership mechanism
needs a fresh scoped admission and a frozen same-contract test. A separately
requested engineering comparison may examine useful acceleration without
claiming a new research mechanism. This closure does not authorize it.

Before any future population, resolve the capacity gray interval/precedence,
exact-versus-rounded keeper cutoff, training-only Block32 derivation, duplicate
candidate/tie/layout conventions, FP64 lower-bound legality and full-score
qualification. The clarification proposals were not applied to a candidate in
this phase. No GPU, remote host, dependencies or manuscript were changed.
