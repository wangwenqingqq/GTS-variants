## Separate decisions

- **Implementation:** correct under tested outputs/fixtures; nine serial CPU
  processes, no experimental failures/replacements. Current membership agrees
  exactly with frozen deterministic selections; launch-time small-input
  attestation remains missing and disclosed.
- **Mechanism:** this query-ball/cardinality-split/source-annulus combination
  fails the necessary opportunity gate. It does not lower distance work or
  frontier against strong simple cached-GTS on the primary samples.
- **Thesis:** stop promotion; do not write CUDA or claim a new GTS++ contribution
  from classical shared frontiers. This does not close unrelated update/ownership
  mechanisms or every query-access-compatible schedule.
- **Confidence:** exact integer decisions on declared samples, with CPU,
  derived-data, native-baseline and provenance limitations; not a population
  theorem or physical GPU measurement.
- **Reopen only if:** a distinct prior-art-cleared mechanism supplies a frozen
  same-contract reason to beat cached-GTS/metric joins by an order of magnitude,
  including its construction, ownership and update costs. Do not tune observed
  samples, relabel proxies, or revive the claim using a weak literal baseline.
